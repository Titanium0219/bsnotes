Ek Baat Jo Shayad Main Kabhi Puri Tarah Keh Nahi Paya...

Maybe bahut kuch change ho gaya hai... shayad ab hamare rishte ko woh naam nahi raha jo kabhi hua karta tha...

Lekin sach bolu to, kuch log sirf rishte ka naam change ho jane se apni jagah nahi badal dete. Tum aaj bhi meri life ka woh part ho jisko explain karna mere liye kabhi easy nahi raha.

Na tum mere liye koi normal person ho... aur na hi hamare bich jo kuch bhi tha, ya jo aaj bhi hai, usse kuch words me explain kiya ja sakta hai.

Kabhi kabhi sochta hu ki hamare bich itna kuch hone ke baad bhi, aaj bhi tumhari importance meri life me waise hi hai... shayad pehle se alag hai, lekin kam nahi hui.

Bas itna janta hu ki chahe kitna bhi time beet jaye... chahe hamari life kitni bhi change ho jaye... tumhari jagah mere andar hamesha alag hi rahegi.

Ho sakta hai kal ko hamari life aur bhi alag direction me chali jaye... ho sakta hai ham pehle ki tarah har baat share na kare... ho sakta hai waqt ke saath bahut kuch badal jaye...

Lekin kuch memories... kuch baatein... aur kuch log... waqt ke saath purane zaroor ho jate hain, lekin unki value kabhi kam nahi hoti.

Aur tum unhi logon me se ho.

Aaj tumhara birthday hai... aur maine ye chhota sa surprise isliye banaya hai kyuki tum mere liye aaj bhi bahut special ho.

Itni special ki tumhara birthday bas normally nikal jaye aur main kuch na karu... ye mujhe acha nahi laga.

Shayad main har baar tumhe ye feel nahi kara pata ki tum meri life me kitni important ho... shayad kuch baatein kabhi boli bhi nahi jaati... lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki woh importance kabhi kam ho gayi.

Pata nahi ye sab dekh kar tum kya feel karogi... lekin bas dil se itna chahta hu ki ise dekh kar tumhare face par ek smile aaye...

Aur tumhe ek baar ye zaroor feel ho... ki chahe aaj hamare rishte ka koi naam ho ya na ho... tum meri life me kabhi "bas koi" nahi ban sakti. ❤️

Kuch rishte waqt ke saath badal jate hain... kuch rishte apna naam kho dete hain... lekin kuch log... chahe jitni door chale jayein, dil me unki jagah kabhi koi aur nahi le pata.

Aur shayad... tum mere liye hamesha unhi logon me se ek rahogi. ❤️✨
